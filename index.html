Shuki Calendar Sync

A personal tool, used by its author only.

Shuki Calendar Sync copies to-dos that have a date and a time from the author's own notes into a single calendar in the author's own Google Calendar, called "Shuki To-Dos". It runs once a night on the author's computer.

It only uses Google's narrowest calendar permission, which lets it see and change calendars it created itself. It cannot see or change any other calendar.

It is not offered to anyone else, and there is nothing to sign up for.
